### **Situation**
Shield Insurance faced challenges in tracking and visualizing their overall performance due to fragmented data sources and limited reporting capabilities.  
Decision-makers lacked a consolidated view of policy sales, revenue, and customer behavior across different regions and channels.

### **Task**
To design a **Power BI dashboard** that:
- Consolidates sales, revenue, and customer data.
- Provides actionable insights for strategic decisions.
- Highlights performance trends and areas of improvement.

### **Action**
- Collected and cleaned data from multiple sources.
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with filters, slicers, and visuals.
- Applied DAX functions for KPIs such as total revenue, policy growth rate, and claim ratios.
- Implemented visual storytelling using bookmarks and conditional formatting for better insights.

### **Result**
- Created a **user-friendly and interactive dashboard** providing real-time business intelligence.
- Enabled management to **track performance KPIs** and identify top-performing products, regions, and agents.
- Improved decision-making efficiency by **30%** (hypothetical example if you wish to include a result metric).

---

## 🎯 **Objectives**
- To analyze **insurance sales performance** by region, channel, and product type.
- To identify **top-performing agents** and **high-revenue products**.
- To examine **claims ratio and customer segmentation** for operational insights.
- To provide a **real-time visual report** for management review.

---

## 📊 **Key Insights**
- **Revenue Concentration:** Most of the revenue was generated from specific regions/products.
- **Customer Trends:** Certain age groups showed higher policy purchase rates.
- **Claim Patterns:** Higher claim ratios were observed in selected regions.
- **Sales Channel Efficiency:** Online channels outperformed traditional sales methods.

## 🔍 **Dashboard Features**
- 📈 Dynamic KPIs: Total Premium, Total Claims, Claim Ratio, Policy Count.
- 🌍 Interactive Filters: Region, Product, Policy Type, Channel.
- 📅 Time Intelligence: Year-over-Year (YoY) and Month-over-Month (MoM) trend comparisons.
- 🎨 Custom Visuals: Conditional formatting, data bars, and DAX-driven measures.
- 📑 Bookmarks for storytelling and quick insights navigation.

## 🧮 **KPIs & Metrics**
| KPI | Description |
|-----|--------------|
| Total Premium | Sum of all premium amounts collected |
| Total Claims | Number and value of insurance claims |
| Claim Ratio | (Total Claims / Total Premium) × 100 |
| Active Policies | Count of policies currently in force |
| Revenue Growth | Month-over-Month and Year-over-Year change |
